# CHATCTBMF

### Human-in-the-Loop Clinical AI Evaluation for Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

CHATCTBMF is an independent Clinical AI initiative focused on the structured evaluation of AI-generated clinical reasoning in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Hospital Dentistry, facial trauma, emergency care, and related dental domains.

The project explores how large language models and other AI systems can support clinical reasoning while maintaining specialist human oversight, evidence-based decision-making, and patient safety.

## Clinical Domains

CHATCTBMF focuses on the evaluation of AI outputs involving:

- Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery
- Facial trauma and emergency care
- Hospital Dentistry
- Intensive Care Dentistry
- Oral diagnosis and differential diagnosis
- Temporomandibular Disorders and Orofacial Pain
- Craniofacial imaging interpretation
- Surgical treatment planning
- Postoperative management
- Medically complex patients
- General clinical dentistry

## Clinical AI Evaluation Framework

AI-generated clinical responses may be assessed across several dimensions:

1. Clinical reasoning
2. Diagnostic accuracy
3. Differential diagnosis
4. Identification of red flags
5. Treatment planning
6. Procedural justification
7. Evidence quality
8. Patient safety
9. Clinical documentation
10. Recognition of uncertainty and limitations

The objective is not simply to determine whether an AI answer is factually correct, but whether its reasoning is clinically appropriate, safe, evidence-based, and useful in a real-world healthcare environment.

## Human-in-the-Loop Model

The framework is designed around structured human clinical review.

A typical evaluation workflow may include:

**Clinical Case / Prompt**

↓

**AI-Generated Response**

↓

**Independent Clinical Review**

↓

**Identification of Reasoning, Evidence, or Safety Issues**

↓

**Specialist Adjudication**

↓

**Corrected / Reference Clinical Answer**

↓

**Structured Feedback for AI Evaluation**

This approach allows comparison between AI-generated reasoning and expert clinical judgment.

## Multi-Reviewer Evaluation

CHATCTBMF also explores multi-reviewer evaluation models.

Postgraduate dental residents may independently evaluate selected AI-generated clinical responses using standardized criteria.

Their assessments can then be compared for inter-reviewer agreement and subsequently reviewed through specialist adjudication.

This creates a potential framework for scalable human-in-the-loop evaluation while maintaining specialist oversight.

## Research and Clinical Translation

The project is informed by experience in:

- Hospital-based clinical practice
- Postgraduate residency education
- Clinical research supervision
- Evidence review
- Facial trauma management
- Intensive care dentistry
- Digital surgical planning
- 3D craniofacial reconstruction
- AI-assisted clinical information analysis

A central principle of CHATCTBMF is translating clinical evidence and structured evaluation into practical improvements in healthcare decision-making.

## Patient Safety

Patient safety is treated as a primary evaluation dimension.

AI outputs should be assessed not only for correctness, but also for potentially harmful omissions, inappropriate recommendations, failure to identify emergencies, unsupported certainty, and other clinically significant errors.

AI is considered a decision-support and evaluation tool, not a replacement for qualified healthcare professionals.

## Data and Privacy

No identifiable patient information is intended for public repositories.

Public examples should use synthetic, anonymized, or appropriately de-identified clinical scenarios designed for educational and AI-evaluation purposes.
